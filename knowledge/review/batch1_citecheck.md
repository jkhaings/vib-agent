**Every vibration observation in batch1 is NOT_FOUND. Every oil and thermal
observation is PARTIAL at best. Not one is SUPPORTED.**

The cause is structural, not clerical. **#01, #02 and #04 are dismounted-bearing
visual damage atlases.** They are photo plates with cause captions and Actions
bullets. They contain no vibration spectra, no fault frequencies, no envelope
analysis, no particle counts and no ferrography. #03 is the only cited source in
batch1 with any vibration content at all, and only two entries cite it.

So batch1 was authored by writing vibration, oil and thermal discriminators from
general engineering knowledge and attaching them to entries whose citations are
visual guides. Nothing in the file *says* those observations are sourced — but
they sit in the same block as the citations, unmarked, which is exactly the
authority-by-adjacency the `inferred:true` flag was added in R2-B0.1 to prevent.
One observation got that flag. Fifteen more needed it.

This is the highest-class finding of the R2 effort so far, and it was invisible
to every check that came before: the schema validator passes (citations resolve),
the entries read as competent, and the notes are broadly faithful. Only reading
the pages against the claims exposes it.
